Lorenzo Casprini
PhD Candidate in Economics, Università di Bologna

I am a 3rd-year PhD candidate at the Department of Economics, University of Bologna. I am currently a Visiting Researcher at the Migration and Sustainable Development (MIG) group at the International Institute for Applied Systems Analysis (IIASA), in Laxenburg, Vienna.

My research focuses on climate-induced migration, with a particular interest in water depletion as a push factor. I study how irrigation, a short-run adaptation to water stress, can become a long-run maladaptation, and how this shapes migration decisions over time. I combine empirical and theoretical approaches.

You can reach me at lorenzo.casprini@unibo.it or connect with me on LinkedIn.
