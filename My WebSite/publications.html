  • Climate Migration in Europe? Evidence from Drought Exposure and Internal Migration in Italy Under Review
    Emre Karabulutoğlu, Lorenzo Casprini, Raya Muttarak
    Under Review · 2025

    This paper investigates the causal relationship between drought exposure and internal migration decisions in Italy, leveraging quasi-experimental variation in precipitation-based drought indices across provinces. The analysis contributes evidence on climate-induced mobility in a Southern European context, with implications for future population dynamics under projected warming scenarios.

    Preprint Abstract ↓ Slides
    Using annual bilateral migration flow data from ISTAT for 104 Italian provinces (2002–2023) combined with drought severity indices (SPEI and PDSI), we examine how worsening climatic conditions affect internal migration within Italy. We find robust evidence that drought exposure drives internal migration flows — even in a high-income country with greater adaptive capacity. Drought-induced out-migration is strongest in middle-income regions, and foreign citizens display a markedly higher propensity to migrate than Italian citizens in response to adverse climatic conditions.
