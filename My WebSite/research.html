  • Climate Migration in Europe? Evidence from Drought Exposure and Internal Migration in Italy Under Review
    Emre Karabulutoğlu, Lorenzo Casprini, Raya Muttarak · 2025
    Draft Abstract ↓
    Using annual bilateral migration flow data from ISTAT for 104 Italian provinces (2002–2023) combined with drought severity indices (SPEI and PDSI), we examine how worsening climatic conditions affect internal migration within Italy. We find robust evidence that drought exposure drives inter-provincial migration flows, even in a high-income country with greater adaptive capacity. Drought-induced out-migration is strongest in middle-income provinces, and foreign citizens display a markedly higher propensity to migrate than Italian citizens in response to adverse climatic conditions.
