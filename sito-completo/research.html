  • Drought Exposure and Internal Migration in Italy
    with E. Karabulutoğlu and R. Muttarak
    Nature Communications, conditionally accepted
    Draft Abstract ↓
    Research on climate-induced migration has focused mainly on low- and middle-income countries. We ask whether such patterns also emerge in a high-income setting: Italy. Combining ISTAT bilateral migration flows across 104 provinces (2002–2023) with drought severity indices (SPEI and PDSI), we find robust evidence that drought exposure drives inter-provincial migration, with cumulative effects exceeding 560,000 migrants in high-exposure settings, and also raises international out-migration. Foreign citizens respond more strongly than Italian citizens, and effects are largest in middle-income regions rather than in the poorest or richest areas.
